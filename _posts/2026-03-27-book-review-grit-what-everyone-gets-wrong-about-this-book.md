Ever feel like you’re pushing a boulder uphill, day after day, year after year, only to find yourself no closer to the summit? You're working hard, you're dedicated, you're *gritty* – at least, that's what you tell yourself. But sometimes, despite all that effort, something still feels off.

Maybe you're burnt out, or stuck in a cycle of relentless striving without a clear destination. You've heard about "grit," the buzzword that promises success through sheer perseverance. You pick up Angela Duckworth's acclaimed book, *Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance*, hoping for the secret sauce.

And you find it. Or do you? What many people misunderstand about grit – and what Stoicism helps clarify – is that it's not just about pushing harder. It's about pushing *smarter*, with a clear purpose, and knowing when to adjust your course, all while maintaining an unshakeable inner resolve.
